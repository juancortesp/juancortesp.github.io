About me

From digital and performance marketing to data analysis

I'm a Data Analyst based in Valladolid, Spain. I come from digital and performance marketing, where I spent years defining KPIs, building dashboards and analysing business performance and where it could improve.

I started in Business Intelligence at Self Bank, working with SQL and Power BI. I then spent several years evaluating investment performance across different markets, sectors and clients.

In the end, both worlds share the same core: turning a business problem into a measurable question. I enjoy it when that question becomes a well-written query, a model you can explain and a chart anyone can understand.
